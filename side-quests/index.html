01 / Quest log

How the story goes

  1. Ch. 1
    A world of imaginationOnce upon a time, everything in this world was just imagination. Protons, atoms, things no one could show me: I had to picture it all in my mind, with nothing real to touch.
  2. Ch. 2
    The first dungeonThen I found a dungeon. After clearing it, I was rewarded with an ancient book written in an ancient language: code. For the first time, what I imagined answered back. Cast a spell, watch it work. Break it, see exactly what broke.
  3. Ch. 3
    Beginner mageI started learning the spells inside it and became a beginner mage. My early experiments were reckless: Android ROMs and kernels, then my first apps.
  4. Ch. 4
    Choosing a classAs time went on, I kept exploring and trained in every class. In this world they go by other names: the Mage is Backend, the Knight is Frontend, and the Blacksmith is DevOps.
  5. Ch. 5
    Country-level mageToday I am a country-level Mage: my spells run production systems that real people depend on. My sword arm holds its own, and I can still forge my own gear when the party needs it. The adventure continues.
